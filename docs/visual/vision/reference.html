Human-guided workflow evaluation

Turn an explicitly authorized workflow into a testable candidate. Preserve the owner, evidence and permission boundary from selection through rollback.

Design reference. This page shows proposed workflows. Measured results and serving approval are unavailable.

Refinement starts with permission

Choose a repeatable pattern after its owner opts in. Keep source permission, quality evidence and serving approval visible throughout the review.

Immutable output feedback

Bind feedback to the exact output ID, revision and digest. Edits create a new version.

Gold eligibility

Use reviewed examples under explicit data-use permission and versioned eligibility rules.

Serving approval

Approve the exact candidate and rollback reference under a separate release decision.

Where the idea might apply

Repeatable assistance

Compare opted-in support triage or document tagging after checking source permission and acceptance criteria.

Agent coordination

Study bounded task routing under explicit review. Scheduling, credential grants and session control require their own permission.

Opportunity research

Study how to evaluate job or contract opportunities. Contact, submissions and commitments require separate authorization.

Human judgment, relationships, legal duties and physical work shape where these proposals apply.
